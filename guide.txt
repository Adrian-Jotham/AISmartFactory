[Notice] Team Presentation Schedule and Guidelines

Team presentations will begin in Week 5, on Tuesday, September 29. Please check the attached file for your presentation schedule.

■ Topics and Presentation Format

You may choose your topic freely, as long as it is related to fault diagnosis or predictive maintenance in smart factories.

- Part I: Fault Diagnosis  
  Choose one topic related to a machine, facility, sensor, communication system, or another part of a smart factory. 
  Explain the possible faults and how they can be diagnosed. 
  If possible, choose a topic related to your team members’ fields of study. AI/ML is not required for Part I.

- Part II: Predictive Maintenance with AI  
  Build on your Part I topic. Explain how condition data can be used to predict faults and support maintenance decisions.

  Each team should work toward one shared topic and goal, with clear roles for all members. Presentation time is about 10 minutes per person.

■ Submission of Presentation Slides

Please email your slides to kyhwang@kumoh.ac.kr by the day before your presentation. The slides will be posted on LMS before the presentation.

■ Evaluation and Class Participation

Parts I and II will be evaluated together on a relative grading basis. Practical implementation using a dataset, simulation, or data analysis will be reflected in the “Technical Depth & Application” evaluation item.

Please also listen carefully to other teams and ask questions when you are curious about their work. 
During presentations, avoid sleeping, using your phone for unrelated activities, or leaving the room without a valid reason. 
Attentive listening, questions, and overall class participation will be reflected in your participation grade.

■ Additional Credit: Journal Submission

If your team submits research related to its presentation topic to a domestic or international journal, including a Scopus-indexed journal, I will review the paper and proof of submission. 
After confirmation, the team will receive the highest score for the assignment component.

Please also submit a final report and, if applicable, your implementation or analysis materials. If you have any questions, email me at the address above.
