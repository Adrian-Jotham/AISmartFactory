    About the data
    • Steps 2 to 5 use the real recording of NASA/IMS test 2, processed by our Part I pipeline (15-minute medians). Alarm times and the diagnosis are the pipeline's own results.
    • The replacement in step 5 is a what-if: our rule applied to the recording. In the real test nobody replaced bearing 1, so the recording keeps rising until it breaks.
    • Steps 6 and 7 cover all 12 bearings of the three tests. Planned replacement = 1, breakdown = 10, the crew needs 24 h, and the fixed schedule is the best one chosen with hindsight. Data: pipeline/replay.py.
